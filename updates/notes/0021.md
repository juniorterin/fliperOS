- The network use in the FliperOS menu moved to the bottom line, at the left of the dots and the version.
- **Rebooting or powering off from the menu no longer hangs about 90 s** on the reboot screen: the menu lets go of the screen first, and whatever is left in the session gets 10 s to close.
- **A panel or controller that shows up as an Xbox 360 pad no longer presses two buttons at once in RetroArch** (button 2 also pressed 5 in MAME 2010, on both players). The saved mapping is rewritten by this update.
