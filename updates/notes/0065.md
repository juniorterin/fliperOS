- Choose the FliperOS version: Setup > System > FliperOS version, and System > FliperOS version in the web panel, list the updates from the one your cabinet's image came with to the newest. Pick an earlier one to go back if an update broke something (the files it replaces are copied first), or a newer one to go forward. On an earlier one the cabinet stays there: the boot no longer asks and nothing is installed at power off, until a newer update comes out. "Follow the updates" goes back to the newest.
- The power button closes a GroovyMAME game at once, and MAME saves the high scores, NVRAM and settings as it does with Esc. Before, it ignored the button and only closed 5 seconds later, without saving.
- A keyboard, panel or controller plugged in after the cabinet starts now works with the power and volume keys. Before, only the ones present at boot did.
- Midway 2D games (Mortal Kombat 1 to 3, NBA Jam, Smash TV, NARC and the others on the Y, T, W and X-unit boards) no longer look stretched up and down on the tube: GroovyMAME runs them in 320x240p at 59 Hz, the same height as the other games, instead of their 400x254 at 54.7 Hz.
