- NVIDIA cards: Setup > Video Setup > Video driver can switch from nouveau to NVIDIA's own driver, downloaded from Ubuntu (driver 580, from the GTX 900 series on; the GTX 600 and 700 stay on nouveau) and compiled for the 15 kHz kernel. It is the driver Batocera uses for native 320x240 on these cards, and it also runs 3D at full speed. The switch is on trial: confirm the picture after the restart, or FliperOS goes back to nouveau by itself; the boot menu also has a "nouveau driver" entry. After the picture is confirmed, the Setup offers the native low resolution test again. Not seen on an NVIDIA card yet.
