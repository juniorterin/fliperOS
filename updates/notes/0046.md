- Frontend list: **RECENT** (the last 10 games played) and **FAVOURITES** groups at the very start of the list. They open and close like a game with clones (button 1; button 2 on a game inside closes it), follow the romlist and its filter, and do not show when they have no games. The vertical list also shows the play counter (PLAYED nX) and the FAV tag under the icons.
- PCSX2x6 (Namco System 246/256): the arcade buttons now follow Setup > Joysticks > Button mapping and Buttons layout. Button N of a game goes to the panel position the layout gives it (position N without a layout), on the controller of each player; before it used the PS2 default layout, which left some buttons on places the panel does not have. The update writes the new buttons into the emulator's settings; a change made inside PCSX2x6 is replaced by the next Button mapping save.
- Setup > Joysticks > Button mapping: a light gun (GunCon 2) is no longer counted as a player, so "How many players?" starts on the right number.
- Setup > Joysticks > GunCon 2 > Calibrate: with more than one gun, it asks how many players have a gun and calibrates each one in turn (the gun that shoots first is the player's); each gun keeps its range by USB port, and a gun not calibrated yet keeps the general one.
