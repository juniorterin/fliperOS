- The system strip of the FliperOS frontend shows how many games each system has as you pass over it, before its list opens. The numbers are kept from the last time, so they are there right after the frontend opens.
- Button 1 on the system strip opens the system at once. Stopping on a system still opens it, with a shorter wait before the switch animation (0.8 s instead of 1.6 s).
- The lists of the other systems are read in the background after the frontend opens, so switching systems no longer waits for the folder to be read.
