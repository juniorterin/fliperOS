- No more false "Video card changed" question after a boot. The check ran before the video driver had loaded (an R9 380's amdgpu came 1 s later), saw only the firmware's framebuffer and took the same card for a new one. It now looks at the PCI bus, where the card is from power-on. Cabinets that already got the question lose it by themselves, without asking.
- Custom games: nothing stays running after the game. Each PortProton game closed with the exit shortcut left two Wine processes behind (pv-adverb and winedevice.exe), one more pair per game, until a reboot. The game's runner now adopts whatever the game leaves and closes it when the game ends.
- On cards that only do super resolution: OpenBOR and Ikemen GO games (KOF Final Attack, and custom games with save/config.ini) now stretch to fill the wide mode. MUGEN custom games get the wide mode too, but MUGEN 1.0 keeps 4:3 and looks narrow. Those and every other game that doesn't fill the screen show an orange "VIDEO CARD WARNING" on the on-screen menu bar for 14 s. The 240p hack is turned off on these cards (it left the tube black). On a card that makes the mode, the next launch puts the game's settings back.
