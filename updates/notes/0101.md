- The Midway 2D games (Mortal Kombat 1 to 3, NBA Jam, Smash TV, NARC and the others on the Y, T, W and X-unit boards) go back to how GroovyMAME opens them by default: their own mode, 400x254 at 54.7 Hz, no longer forced to 320x240p at 59 Hz.
