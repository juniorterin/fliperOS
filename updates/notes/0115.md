- **APUs with the VGA on the motherboard boot with a picture again.** Update 114 forced the VGA output on at boot. On the AMD APUs whose VGA comes from a DisplayPort bridge chip (Kaveri, Trinity and Richland: A4, A6, A8 and A10 with the VGA on the motherboard), that left the screen black with any monitor, a PVM too: the forced output skips the step where the system reads the bridge chip. This update takes the force out on those machines, and the output is detected at boot as before. Radeon cards with a VGA of their own keep it.
- **If your cabinet shows nothing since update 114:** connect the PVM or a TV, turn it on and press a key in the boot menu, press **e** on the FliperOS entry, delete the **e** at the end of `video=VGA-1:...` (`320x240Se` becomes `320x240S`) and press **F10**. FliperOS starts, and this update makes it permanent.
