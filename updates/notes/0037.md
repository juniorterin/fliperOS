- Link play in the frontend, on the network cable only: the games that link cabinets (Daytona USA, Scud Race, Daytona USA 2, Dirt Devils, Club Kart: European Session, Super Street Fighter II Tournament Battle) get a green globe in the list and LINK PLAY or JOIN LINK in the game menu, with a waiting room that starts the machines in the right order. Each cabinet number gets its own NVRAM, and yours comes back after the game.
- Net play for the FBNeo games that have more players than the cabinet has controls (X-Men has four): NET PLAY in the game menu. Each cabinet takes as many players as it has controls configured.
- The number of players follows the connected controllers: Setup > Joysticks says how many are connected, and "How many players?" starts on that number.
- On-screen menu: each resolution has its own geometry, and the Geometry page says which one it is adjusting.
- PCSX2 opens the game directly when the frontend starts it, without the Big Picture first.
- Web panel: the login screen is cleaner.
