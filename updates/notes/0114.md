- **Arcade monitor on VGA: a picture from the boot.** A VGA or DVI-I output without an EDID is now always forced on at boot. The video card sees a monitor there only by the 75 Ω load on the colour lines: a PVM or a TV has it, many arcade monitors (often behind a J-PAC) don't. So a cabinet set up on a PVM booted black on the arcade monitor (the output stayed "disconnected", with no picture even in the menus), and worked only when the PC started on the PVM first. This update adds the force to the boot line of the cabinets set up that way, keeping the resolution you chose. It takes effect at the next boot.
