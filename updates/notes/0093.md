- HBMAME is now a system of its own, next to GroovyMAME (hbgroovymame): the arcade hacks and homebrews of HBMAME, in the folder ~/roms/hbmame (it is there from now on, like the others). It does not come in the image: the first time you open one of its games from the frontend, FliperOS downloads and installs HBMAME by itself (a package of about 50 MB, with the progress bar; it can also be installed in Setup > Games > Emulators & Games). Like GroovyMAME, each game opens in its own video mode on the CRT, with the audio kept in step with the video, party play on 2 or more cabinets, multi-screen games on several monitors, and the high scores on the Pixelcade, in the web panel and in the cloud backup. The monitor, the picture format, the latency and the button mapping of the Setup apply to it too. It keeps its own data in ~/.hbmame, ~/nvram/hbmame, ~/save/hbmame and ~/bios/hbmame (the BIOS sets in ~/bios/mame work for it too), and it shows up in every frontend as HBMAME. Tested with test ROMs in a virtual environment, not yet on a CRT.
- The full screen of the frontend has a new look: in place of the column of thumbnails, a wheel of logos on the right, like the cards of a rotary file, five at a time (seven on a vertical monitor), the chosen one in the middle and bigger, the others tilted in perspective, the first and the last fading out; a game with no logo gets a 4:3 card with its title. After 2 seconds without touching the controls the cards turn edge-on and disappear, the game's logo shows above its name and a small neon arcade ball-top moves on its own in the corner; the first touch brings the wheel back. Not seen yet on a CRT.
- In the grid and in full screen the clones are grouped: a game with clones shows once, with a badge saying how many versions it has. Button 1 opens a dialog with all of them (MAIN SET and one line per clone), up and down move, button 1 picks one and opens its game menu (PLAY, star, +), and button 2 closes it. The list works as before. Not seen yet on a CRT.
- The music of the frontend now starts with a random song each time it opens, never the one that played last (before, Retrowave always came first).
