- Web panel, System area redesigned, lighter: the tabs are plain text with a line under the open one (on a phone, the end with more tabs fades and an arrow blinks slowly), and the pages sit in one column next to the side bar instead of filling a wide screen.
- System > Setup is a list of settings like a phone's Settings: the groups in a column with a search over every setting, and the open group in sections with one row per setting (the name on the left; a switch, side-by-side buttons, a list or a slider on the right; the explanation behind the ⓘ). On a phone the groups are the first screen. The settings have Portuguese names in Portuguese.
- The steps on the tube are rows marked "on the cabinet", with Watch it live in the question before opening them.
- Video starts with the video card in use and its driver; with a card that takes two drivers, "change on the cabinet" opens the choice on the tube.
- Interlaced hacks: picking Filter for a program shows the filter level on the same row.
- Video > On-screen menu lists the profiles saved in the on-screen menu (game, core, emulator, resolution, default) with what each one changes; a profile can be deleted from the panel.
- Audio > Menu sounds: send the two sounds of the Setup menu (cursor and Enter) as .wav files from the browser, play, replace or remove them.
- Status: the tube display with the band, mode, output and driver; each CPU core as a VU meter column; temperatures, memory, disks and network as compact bars.
- RetroAchievements, Cloud backup and Lab mode use the same rows; custom games have smaller tiles with their buttons over the picture, and a lighter table.
