- **Network folder: play the games of a NAS, RetroNAS or PC.** Setup > Games > Folders > External storage > **Network folder** (and the web panel, System > Setup > Games, where the IP and the password are easier to type) asks for the computer, your user and password, lists its shared folders and opens the one you pick at every boot in /media/fliperos, like one more disk. The games are played from there: nothing is copied. A RetroNAS, or the share of a Batocera, with batocera/roms/<console> goes straight to the Batocera filesystem, and each console becomes a system of the frontends. It does what a cifs line in /etc/fstab did on other systems, without editing that file (the desktop's editor can't save it). A computer that is off is skipped after a 2-second check, instead of each folder waiting for it, and a NAS that starts after the cabinet works without a restart. The ROM Cleaner lists the saved network folders too.
- **FliperOS Matchmaker, a first look (read only).** A separate program for online games, in Setup > Games > Frontend: one page per emulator (GroovyMAME, Flycast, the RetroArch cores with net play) with who is online now, and the lobby of each game (looking for a match, playing, in a party, Wi-Fi or cable, ping). The cabinet is online only while it is open. Matches come in the next updates.
