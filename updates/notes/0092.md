- The three songs the frontend plays now come with FliperOS: "BurtySounds - Retrowave", "Musinova - Information Flow" and "Kaazoom - Looking For A Way Back", from Pixabay, distributed with the authors' authorization (the credits are in the README). Each one is copied to ~/media/fliperos-sounds/music the first time the Setup or the frontend opens, so a cabinet with no songs starts with music (the first one to play is "BurtySounds - Retrowave"). A song that is already there is left as it is, and one you delete does not come back. This update brings about 14 MB, and the new ISO has them too.
