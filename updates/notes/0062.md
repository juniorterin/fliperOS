- A DisplayPort converter that shows native 15 kHz modes keeps them. On a cabinet with a 15 kHz monitor on DisplayPort, the next boot asks once to test this converter: a test grid is shown, and if you can see it, the games go back to native resolutions as before update 60. If not, the super resolutions stay. The test is also in Setup > Video Setup > Low resolutions, and in the video output test of the installation media.
- The frontend plays CDs and DVDs from a drive connected to the cabinet: a PHYSICAL MEDIA row under the systems finds the console from the disc, and its card has PLAY (straight from the drive), EMULATOR and RIP TO FOLDER (a .chd or .iso in the games folder). Setup > Games > Frontend > Boot from media opens on the drive. Not yet tried with a real drive.
