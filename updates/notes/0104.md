- **Sega Chihiro**, a new system, with the xemu emulator (Chihiro build). Put the game disc in `~/roms/chihiro` (a `.7z` from the TOSEC set, a `.cue`, a `.gdi` or a `.chd`) and open it from the frontend. The first time, FliperOS installs the emulator (about 40 MB, it is not in the image), reads the disc, downloads only that game's key file from the MAME romset with the link of the Downloader (Setup > Games > Downloader, or the one the image ships) and builds the game image, with a progress bar. After that the game opens at once. It has its own logo, video through Vulkan on AMD cards (OpenGL froze the GPU of a Radeon R7 240), sound, the CPU and GPU speed-ups of the emulator and the buttons of your panel or pad (Setup > Joysticks > Button mapping: coin, start, stick, b1 to b7), and in the gun games the GunCon 2 buttons and a shot off the screen no longer open the emulator's menu. Seen on a cabinet with Virtua Cop 3; the other Chihiro games use the same code but were not tried.
- Frontend: PLAY no longer turns the TV off and then shows the progress bar when the game still needs something to open (the emulator, a core, the unpacking of a Namco game, the disc of a Chihiro game). It goes straight to the bar. A game that opens at once still plays the animation.
- Desktop: the Setup opens at 240p on the tube. The screen scrolls with the controller pointer when a window is bigger than it. Double-clicking a `GAME.fliperos-customgame.zip` installs the game, and the package has its own icon. Custom games get an optional **Icon** (Create package, in the Setup and in the web panel) and are listed once in the Games menu. The on-screen keyboard only comes up on a click.
- Boot splash on a 31 kHz monitor: the text theme is drawn in the 640x480 the tube shows, so the title and the bar are no longer cut off on the right and at the bottom. The boot image is rebuilt by this update.
- Odin (Sega Hikaru, Gaelco 3D): cabinets that already had the factory 320x240 line now open it at 640x480 (the emulator's window does not go below that, and only a quarter of the picture showed).
- Updated packages: **Dolphin 2609b** (with the net play patch) and **RAHasher 1.8.5**.
