- **A Batocera disk on ext4 no longer stops the updates.** Its saves and nvram folders belonged to root, so FliperOS could not create the RetroArch saves folder in them: RetroArch saved nothing and update 109 stopped with "cannot change owner and permissions of /opt/fliperos/saves/retroarch". The folders now belong to the FliperOS user, on a disk that is already in use too, and a link to a folder that does not exist yet no longer stops an update.
