- Frontend, the menu of a game: the button you pick now moves in the games other cabinets play too. PLAY pulses in whole steps (it grows, lights up and goes back) instead of only breathing. In a link game the globe spins (faster when someone has a party open), its nodes blink one at a time, and a wave of energy runs along the row of cabinets, empty places included. In a party game (more players than controls) the two controllers fill four seats one by one, and the players underneath wave. Pressing LINK PLAY, NEW, JOIN or a party button lights it green twice, like PLAY, before it opens the room or joins; a party that already started, or is full, shakes, flashes its border orange and says so, and the menu stays. Not seen yet on a CRT.
- Frontend, the game page (the +): each part comes in one after the other. The data is typed in, the buttons of your panel press down one by one, the cover and the pictures switch on like a tube TV (a dot, a line, then the picture), the stars light up, and the history shows line by line, with the scroll bar lighting up when it scrolls. Switching page slides in steps of 8 pixels (0.16 seconds), and every page plays again each time you come back to it. The old zoom of the page is gone. Sharp steps with no blurred scaling. Not seen yet on a CRT.
