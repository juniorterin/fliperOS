- Lab mode (Setup > System > Lab mode, and the web panel's Settings page): a temporary internet address to the web panel, for someone helping you from far away. Whoever has the address sees the cabinet's status, logs, files and screen and gets a root terminal, without your password, so send it only to someone you trust. It ends by itself after 4 hours, at power off, or when you turn it off. The tunnel is a Cloudflare Quick Tunnel (no account, nothing to change on the router); cloudflared, about 40 MB, is downloaded the first time.
