- Setup > Joysticks > Controllers / Arcade panel is now a menu with Map controllers, Keyboard encoder, Add LPT joysticks, Haptic events and Lights.
- Map controllers: eight player slots (Player 1 to 8), two per line. To give a slot a controller, select the empty card and hold a button on that controller until the bar fills: the card takes it (if the slot had another controller, the two swap). A controller that is already set up just changes card; one that still needs a mapping or a layout opens its menu to be set up. A card with a controller opens its menu with Enter, where each thing is set on its own: Player, Type (arcade panel or joypad), Visual layout, Mapping (all buttons, one button, or the menu buttons), Buttons layout and Test controller. The name of the selected card scrolls.
- The Visual layout of player 1 changes the guide of the frontend by itself (AUTO is the default on the Controls page).
- Test controller: every button, direction, stick and trigger lights up live. Joypads have their own drawing: Xbox, PlayStation, GameCube, Super Nintendo, NES, Dreamcast, Saturn, Mega Drive, Neo Geo CD and Nintendo 64; arcade panels show the stick and the buttons of their layout. Hold Start to leave (or Esc).
- A game now opens with the controllers in the order of the players: when it is out of order (usually the first game after turning the cabinet on), the USB controllers are replugged by software in player order, a couple of seconds, so Flycast and the Flycast Dojo of Fightcade (which give port 1 to the first controller the system lists) follow the player you chose. Bluetooth controllers are not reordered.
- Odin: player 1 and 2 are the controllers you chose in the Setup (before it picked the first two that were connected).
- A controller without a player keeps its mapping, and a mapping can be changed one button at a time.
- Setup > Audio Setup > Stereo or mono: Mono mixes both channels and plays the same sound on both outputs, for a cabinet with a single speaker (the default stays stereo). The installer asks it too, right after the video step, and the web panel has it under Audio > Stereo or mono.
- Audio Setup keeps its icons: it has no summary on top anymore; each item shows its current value (card, volume, stereo or mono, latency) in its hint when the cursor is on it. Long texts in the menus scroll instead of being cut or wrapped into more lines.
