- Intel HD graphics (tested on a first generation, Ironlake): games now open from the FliperOS frontend and the Setup. Before, the screen was not released when a program closed it, so RetroArch and the frontend itself failed to take it back.
- Video Setup > Resolution: on an Intel card there is a new **1280x240 60 Hz progressive** mode (240p, no interlace flicker in the menus). It is made from an EDID of its own and needs a reboot.
- Setup marks what a video card can't run. On a card without OpenGL 3, the item gets a red X, the reason and a question before it opens: EmulationStation-DE (Games > Frontend), 480i hacks (Video Setup), and PCSX2, Dolphin and DuckStation (Games > Emulators & Games).
- EmulationStation-DE and Pegasus are no longer in the image: Setup > Games > Frontend builds the one you choose on the cabinet, with FliperOS's patches (it needs the network and takes a while). Cabinets that already have them keep them.
