- RetroArch now comes as a FliperOS package and is updated over the network like the rest: RetroArch 1.22.2 with FliperOS's Vulkan patch. On a card using the amdgpu driver, RetroArch opens in Vulkan and switches the CRT to each game's own mode (Street Fighter II at 384x224, seen filling the tube on a test cabinet). Before, a cabinet kept the RetroArch of the image it was installed from, and the Vulkan mode switching only came with a new ISO. On other cards RetroArch stays in OpenGL (glcore), as before. Setup > Emulators & Games shows the version this update brings.
