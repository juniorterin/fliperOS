- Samba: the shared folders open again from Windows (\\fliperos\roms, \\fliperos\FliperOS and the others). They follow links out of the share (the custom folders on an external disk), and Samba needs the samba-vfs-modules package for that; without it every share failed (error 0x800704b3 on Windows). This update installs it and restarts Samba.
- Setup > Emulators & Games can build the newest Flycast and RetroArch (the project's latest code, with the FliperOS patches) and take the newest stable Dolphin and the newest GroovyMAME release, right on the cabinet (a new MAME version can change the romset). "Back to the FliperOS package" undoes it, and the updates keep upgrading the package in the meantime.
