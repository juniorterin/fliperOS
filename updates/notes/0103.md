- On-screen menu: "Configuration" is now **Video configuration** (only video settings), and **Controller configuration** is a page of its own, with **GunCon 2** (while the light gun is connected) and **Joystick to keyboard** (the on-screen keyboard of custom games, which used to open straight from the main menu). Page titles that did not fit a very small game screen (160 or 128 pixels wide) now use the small letter instead of being cut off.
- Custom games: new **16:9 crop** for games that only run in a widescreen picture. The game opens in a 16:9 screen and the tube shows only the middle of it, in 4:3, with nothing squeezed and the sides cut off. Turn it on in Create package (web panel and Setup, with an explanation of what it does), when editing a game, or in the on-screen menu > Video configuration > 16:9 crop (it counts the next time the game opens). It is saved in the game's .cfg and travels in its package. A first version: tell us how it looks on your video card.
