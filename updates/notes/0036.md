- DisplayPort and HDMI to VGA converters: a converter reports an EDID of its own, so the output test took the output for an LCD and saved no boot mode, and the menu, the frontend and the screen after leaving a game opened at 48 kHz and the tube lost the picture. With a CRT monitor chosen, the Setup now replaces the converter's EDID with a 15 kHz one and forces the output. **Cabinets already installed keep their video settings.** The new rule applies when you run the output test again, or in Setup > Video Setup > Monitor Type by choosing the same monitor again (then reboot).
- HDMI or DVI-D with a CRT: super resolutions by themselves, even on an AMD card, because that link doesn't go under about 25 MHz.
- On-screen menu over any game or frontend: hold Start and Down for a second (or Pause on a keyboard, Play/Pause on a remote) for volume, the 480i filter, geometry and colour. The volume keys show a bar for 2 seconds.
- The 480i flicker filter and the software saturation and hue now work in Vulkan too: the emulators stay on Vulkan instead of going back to OpenGL.
- Frontend: a Frontend controls area (Tab > Frontend options > Frontend controls) to map buttons and keys, the system logos on the tiles and the top bar, and the list mode with the icons in one row and the synopsis scrolling. Attract-Mode Plus no longer loses the keyboard after Button mapping.
- `~/nvram` has one folder per emulator and core, there is a `~/save` folder, and an update no longer erases the nvram.
- Pixelcade and ZeDMD: the system logo slides in and blinks when you switch lists, each item of the FliperOS menu has its animation, the ZeDMD has a new opening animation and shows the logo when the machine sits idle for 30 seconds.
- Web panel: a Clones button in the game filters, and a marquee preview (Pixelcade or ZeDMD) next to Playing now with the logo, the score or the art.
- Video Setup > Card firmware: the ATOM-15 firmware for AMD cards, with a test at the next boot and a way back to the original BIOS. The real flash has not been run on a card yet.
- The App Store icon is on the desktop.
