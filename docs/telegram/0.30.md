🇺🇸 **FliperOS 0.30: network folder, ROM packages and updates 105 to 116** 🕹️

A new ISO that gathers updates 105 to 116. Cabinets already installed get everything from the updates.

- **Network folder:** the games of a NAS, RetroNAS or another PC are played where they are, as one more disk.
- **Game Packages:** ROM packages with each game's BIOS, art and saves, and packages over 2 GB in parts.
- **Cheats:** a folder in `~/cheats` for each emulator and core, read by itself when the game starts.
- **Versus scoreboard:** first to (FT) on the LED marquee, with the names and the winner with WIN! and fireworks.
- **Arcade monitor on VGA:** a picture from the boot, also behind a J-PAC and with the Setup done on a PVM.

Download the ISO at [FliperOS 0.30](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/releases/tag/0.30). What changed, item by item, is in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

🇧🇷 **Pasta da rede**

Em Setup > Games > Folders > External storage > **Network folder**, ou no painel web (Sistema > Setup > Jogos), diga o computador, o usuário e a senha, e escolha a pasta compartilhada. Ela abre a cada boot em /media/fliperos e os jogos rodam de lá, sem copiar nada. Um RetroNAS ou o compartilhamento de um Batocera vira o Batocera filesystem: cada console é um sistema dos frontends.

🇺🇸 **Network folder**

In Setup > Games > Folders > External storage > **Network folder**, or in the web panel (System > Setup > Games), give the computer, the user and the password, and pick the shared folder. It opens at every boot in /media/fliperos and the games are played from there, nothing copied. A RetroNAS or a Batocera share becomes the Batocera filesystem: each console is a system of the frontends.

🇧🇷 **Pacotes de ROM**

Em Game Packages > Create package > ROMs, escolha um emulador ou core, marque os jogos (com os filtros do frontend e do MAME) e dê um título: sai um `TITULO.fliperos-EMULADOR.zip` com as ROMs e, se você quiser, as BIOS, a arte, a memória, os saves, os cheats e os ajustes. Em outro FliperOS ele instala tudo de volta nas mesmas pastas, e pode trazer um grupo próprio com logotipo. Os pacotes acima de 2 GB saem em partes, do tamanho que você escolher.

🇺🇸 **ROM packages**

In Game Packages > Create package > ROMs, pick an emulator or core, mark the games (with the frontend and MAME filters) and give it a title: out comes a `TITLE.fliperos-EMULATOR.zip` with the ROMs and, if you want, the BIOS, art, memory, saves, cheats and settings. On another FliperOS it installs everything back in the same folders, and it can bring a group of its own with a logo. Packages over 2 GB come out in parts, of the size you choose.

---

🇧🇷 **Cheats e placar FT**

Ponha o arquivo de cheat do jogo na pasta do emulador em `~/cheats` (`mame`, `hbmame`, `ps2`, `flycast`, `dolphin`, `retroarch/<core>`...) e ele é achado quando o jogo abre. No placar versus, escolha first to 1 a 10 e os nomes dos jogadores: quem chega primeiro ganha o nome na cor dele, WIN! piscando e fogos no marquee de LED. No painel web, **JOGAR FT** na página do jogo.

🇺🇸 **Cheats and the FT scoreboard**

Put the game's cheat file in its emulator's folder in `~/cheats` (`mame`, `hbmame`, `ps2`, `flycast`, `dolphin`, `retroarch/<core>`...) and it is found when the game starts. On the versus scoreboard, pick first to 1 to 10 and the players' names: the first to get there has their name in their colour, a blinking WIN! and fireworks on the LED marquee. In the web panel, **PLAY FT** on the game page.
