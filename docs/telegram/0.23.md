🇺🇸 **FliperOS 0.23 is out** 💿

Your original games, straight from the disc or the cartridge: the frontend plays CDs and DVDs from a drive connected to the cabinet, and cartridges from the Open Source Cartridge Reader (OSCR). It finds the console from the disc or the cart itself, lets you pick the emulator or the RetroArch core that reads it, and plays it straight away or rips it to the right folder. The cabinet can even boot straight from the disc, like a console. Also in this ISO: MAME parties with every player of the game, the flicker filter in Wine games, super resolutions for a CRT on DisplayPort and the scanlines in the on-screen menu.

Cabinets already installed get all of this from the Setup's own update (updates 55 to 63). Discs and the cartridge reader have not been tried on real hardware yet: if you have a drive or an OSCR, tell us here how it went.

ISO and the full list of changes: [release 0.23](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/releases/tag/0.23)

🇧🇷 **Um disco no drive**

Com um drive ligado, a tira de sistemas ganha uma seta para baixo: ali fica a fileira PHYSICAL MEDIA, um azulejo por drive. O botão 1 lê o disco, reconhece o console (PlayStation, PS2, Saturn, Sega CD, PC Engine CD, PC-FX, 3DO, Neo Geo CD) e abre o cartão com PLAY, EMULATOR e RIP TO FOLDER. O RetroArch lista só os cores que leem um disco de verdade, e o rip vira um .chd na pasta do emulador.

🇺🇸 **A disc in the drive**

With a drive connected, the system strip gets a down arrow: that is where the PHYSICAL MEDIA row lives, one tile per drive. Button 1 reads the disc, finds the console (PlayStation, PS2, Saturn, Sega CD, PC Engine CD, PC-FX, 3DO, Neo Geo CD) and opens its card with PLAY, EMULATOR and RIP TO FOLDER. RetroArch lists only the cores that read a real disc, and the rip becomes one .chd in the emulator's folder.

🇧🇷 **Cartuchos pelo OSCR**

O Open Source Cartridge Reader HW5 entra na mesma fileira. O Setup > System > Cartridge reader instala nele o firmware do FliperOS (salvando antes o dele), e o frontend passa a ler cada slot pela USB, sempre em 3,3 V enquanto procura. Com dois cartuchos, você escolhe; o PLAY lê o cartucho e abre o jogo, e o RIP TO FOLDER guarda a ROM e o save.

🇺🇸 **Cartridges through the OSCR**

The Open Source Cartridge Reader HW5 joins the same row. Setup > System > Cartridge reader installs the FliperOS firmware on it (saving its own first), and the frontend reads each slot over USB, always at 3.3V while looking. With two carts you pick one; PLAY reads the cart and opens the game, and RIP TO FOLDER keeps the ROM and the save.

🇧🇷 **Ligar direto no disco**

Em Setup > Games > Frontend > Boot from media, o gabinete liga no drive (ou no leitor de cartuchos). Sem disco, a tela pede um; ponha o disco e ele desliza para dentro, é lido, e o cartão espera você apertar PLAY. O botão 2 sempre leva à lista de jogos.

🇺🇸 **Boot straight from the disc**

In Setup > Games > Frontend > Boot from media, the cabinet starts on the drive (or the cartridge reader). With no disc, the screen asks for one; put it in and it slides into the drive, is read, and the card waits for you to press PLAY. Button 2 always goes to the game list.
