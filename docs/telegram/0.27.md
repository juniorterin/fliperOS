🇺🇸 **FliperOS 0.27: sounds and music, folders and disks, and a Games page for custom games** 🎶

A new ISO that gathers updates 86 to 91. Cabinets already installed get everything from the updates.

- **Sounds and music:** the Setup menu and the frontend have six sounds (cursor, Enter, Back, game open, game close and alert), each with its own file and volume, and the frontend plays music with a now-playing bar.
- **Folders and disks:** folder shortcuts, external disks (with formatting as ext4 or exFAT) and disks with a Batocera filesystem used as they are.
- **Custom games:** a Games page in the web panel with editing, group logos and renaming, and many packages at once.
- **Animated menus** in the Setup and in the frontend's game menu (link, party and the + page).
- **Fightcade, Wine and PortProton:** Naomi and Atomiswave games, Wine games that change the video mode, and 32-bit custom games on cards with the Mesa driver.
- Unlocked arcade memory for the MAME 2010 core, USB Wi-Fi adapters that start as a CD-ROM, and FliperOS spelled FliperOS everywhere.

Download the ISO at [FliperOS 0.27](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/releases/tag/0.27). What changed, item by item, is in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

🇧🇷 **Sons no Setup e no frontend**

Setup > Audio Setup > **Menu sounds**: seis sons, cada um com o seu **arquivo** (um seletor de arquivos, em `~/media/fliperos-sounds`) e o seu **volume**. Já vem com sons retrowave: senoides suaves com um eco quente.

No frontend, Tab > FRONTEND SOUNDS é o mesmo conjunto (mudar num lugar muda no outro), mais a música, o volume dela e o do vídeo de preview.

🇺🇸 **Sounds in the Setup and the frontend**

Setup > Audio Setup > **Menu sounds**: six sounds, each with its own **file** (a file picker, in `~/media/fliperos-sounds`) and its own **volume**. It comes with retrowave sounds: soft sine waves with a warm echo.

In the frontend, Tab > FRONTEND SOUNDS is the same set (change one place and the other follows), plus the music, its volume and the preview video's.

🇧🇷 **A música que começou**

Quando uma música começa, entra uma barra com o nome dela, como uma conquista desbloqueada: o círculo com a nota e um cometa girando. A barra tem só a largura do nome e fica longe da borda; um nome comprido corre.

As músicas não vêm no FliperOS (a licença do Pixabay não deixa distribuir o arquivo sozinho): ponha em `~/media/fliperos-sounds/music`. Créditos no README.

🇺🇸 **The song that just started**

When a song starts, a bar with its name slides in, like an unlocked achievement: the circle with a note and a comet running around it. The bar is only as wide as the name and stays clear of the edge; a long name runs.

The songs do not ship with FliperOS (the Pixabay licence does not allow handing the file out on its own): put them in `~/media/fliperos-sounds/music`. Credits are in the README.
