🇺🇸 **FliperOS 0.24 is out** 🎮

Bluetooth controllers, and a Button mapping for joypads too: the Setup pairs, connects and forgets Bluetooth controllers, and the mapping starts from the list of connected controllers, asks the player and maps an arcade panel or JAMMA board (1 to 8 buttons) or a joypad, recognised like in Batocera. The frontend gets a JOYPAD column and a guide with the buttons of ten consoles. Also in this ISO: RetroAchievements in every emulator, cloud backup of scores and saves, the FliperOS version chooser, Midway 2D games in 320x240 at 59 Hz and updates that no longer stop on a repository added by hand.

Cabinets already installed get all of this from the Setup's own update (updates 64 to 66).

ISO and the full list of changes: [release 0.24](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/releases/tag/0.24)

🇧🇷 **Bluetooth e a lista de controles**

Setup > Joysticks > Bluetooth pareia um controle em modo de pareamento (ou todos, por 30 s). No Button mapping, a lista mostra cada controle com o ícone de fio ou sem fio; os com fio vêm sempre primeiro, e os Bluetooth pegam os jogadores que sobram, na ordem em que conectam.

🇺🇸 **Bluetooth and the controller list**

Setup > Joysticks > Bluetooth pairs a controller in pairing mode (or all of them, for 30 s). In Button mapping, the list shows each controller with a wired or wireless icon; wired ones always come first, and Bluetooth ones take the players left, in the order they connect.

🇧🇷 **Arcade ou joypad**

Um painel de arcade ou JAMMA com 1 a 8 botões, ou um joypad com os nomes dos botões dele; no fim, os botões que aceitam e voltam nos menus.

🇺🇸 **Arcade or joypad**

An arcade panel or JAMMA board with 1 to 8 buttons, or a joypad with its own button names; at the end, the buttons that accept and go back in the menus.

🇧🇷 **O guia do joypad**

No frontend, Frontend controls tem a coluna JOYPAD, e o guia desenha os botões de PlayStation, Xbox, GameCube, Super Nintendo, NES, Dreamcast, Saturn, Mega Drive, Neo Geo CD ou Nintendo 64, cada função no botão dela.

🇺🇸 **The joypad guide**

In the frontend, Frontend controls has a JOYPAD column, and the guide draws the buttons of a PlayStation, Xbox, GameCube, Super Nintendo, NES, Dreamcast, Saturn, Mega Drive, Neo Geo CD or Nintendo 64, each function on its button.
