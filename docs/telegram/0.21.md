🇺🇸 **FliperOS 0.21 is out** 🕹️

Namco System 246 and 256 arcade games (Tekken 4 and 5, Soul Calibur II and III, Time Crisis 3 and 4...) now have their own emulator, PCSX2x6, next to PCSX2. Also in this ISO: the BIOS download now comes from RetroBIOS, and Dolphin became a package updated over the network.

Cabinets already installed get all of this from the Setup's own update (updates 40 to 46).

ISO and the full list of changes: [release 0.21](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/releases/tag/0.21)

🇧🇷 **Namco System 246/256: PCSX2x6**

Cada jogo é um arquivo `.acgame` mais a pasta com o `boot.elf` e a imagem, em `~/roms/namco246` ou `~/roms/namco256`. O dongle vai em `~/save`, a BIOS de arcade em `~/bios` e a memória do jogo em `~/nvram`, uma pasta por placa. Os botões do painel seguem o Button mapping do Setup. Os jogos, a BIOS e os dongles não vêm na ISO.

O Tekken 5 abre e joga num gabinete com i7 870, com quedas para 56-57 FPS nas lutas; o processador do console emulado fica em 100%. Dentro do fliperama o gabinete esquentava até 98 °C: uma refrigeração melhor ajuda.

🇺🇸 **Namco System 246/256: PCSX2x6**

Each game is an `.acgame` file plus the folder with its `boot.elf` and image, in `~/roms/namco246` or `~/roms/namco256`. The dongle goes in `~/save`, the arcade BIOS in `~/bios` and the game memory in `~/nvram`, one folder per board. The panel's buttons follow the Setup's Button mapping. The games, BIOS and dongles are not in the ISO.

Tekken 5 boots and plays on a cabinet with an i7 870, dipping to 56-57 FPS in fights; the emulated console's processor sits at 100%. Inside the arcade the cabinet got as hot as 98 C: better cooling helps.

🇧🇷 **BIOS pelo RetroBIOS**

Setup > Games > Downloader > Download BIOS baixa cada arquivo do RetroBIOS com o SHA1 conferido, e o pacote antigo só de reserva. Agora também pede a BIOS de arcade da Namco e a do DuckStation. No menu Tab do frontend, uma caixa BIOS DOWNLOAD mostra o andamento ao lado do AUTO SCRAPE, e as duas saem quando tudo termina.

🇺🇸 **BIOS from RetroBIOS**

Setup > Games > Downloader > Download BIOS takes each file from RetroBIOS with its SHA1 checked, and the old pack only as a reserve. It now also asks for the Namco arcade BIOS and the DuckStation BIOS. In the frontend's Tab menu, a BIOS DOWNLOAD box shows the progress next to AUTO SCRAPE, and both leave when everything is done.
