🇺🇸 **FliperOS 0.28: emulators that install themselves, Map controllers and a new full screen** 🕹️

A new ISO that gathers updates 93 to 99. Cabinets already installed get everything from the updates.

- **Emulators that install themselves:** **HBGroovyMAME** (HBMAME with Switchres), **Odin** (Sega Hikaru and Gaelco 3D) and the **AnyBOR** core (OpenBOR in RetroArch) are not in the image: FliperOS downloads and installs each one the first time you open one of its games.

- **Stereo or mono sound** in the Setup and in the installer, the update number next to the version on the main menu and a Share button on the website.

Download the ISO at [FliperOS 0.28](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/releases/tag/0.28). What changed, item by item, is in the [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/juniorterin/fliperOS/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

🇺🇸 **HBGroovyMAME: HBMAME with Switchres**

HBMAME's hacks and homebrews now have a system of their own, in `~/roms/hbmame`, and each game opens in its own video mode on the CRT, with the audio kept in step with the video, party play on 2 or more cabinets, multi-screen games and the high scores on the Pixelcade, in the web panel and in the cloud backup. It is not in the image: FliperOS installs HBGroovyMAME the first time you open a game from the folder. In the picture, Ultra Street Fighter II (`usf2u`), one of HBMAME's sets, on the select screen.

🇧🇷 **Map controllers**

Em **Setup > Joysticks > Controllers / Arcade panel > Map controllers** há oito vagas de player, duas por linha. Para dar um controle a uma vaga, selecione o cartão vazio e segure um botão naquele controle até a barra encher. Enter em um cartão com controle abre o menu dele: **Player**, **Type**, **Visual layout**, **Mapping**, **Buttons layout** e **Test controller**.

Os jogos abrem com os controles na ordem dos players (o Flycast e o Flycast Dojo dão a porta 1 ao primeiro controle que o Linux lista).

🇺🇸 **Map controllers**

**Setup > Joysticks > Controllers / Arcade panel > Map controllers** has eight player slots, two per line. To give a slot a controller, select the empty card and hold a button on that controller until the bar fills. Enter on a card with a controller opens its menu: **Player**, **Type**, **Visual layout**, **Mapping**, **Buttons layout** and **Test controller**.

Games open with the controllers in player order (Flycast and the Flycast Dojo give port 1 to the first controller Linux lists).

🇧🇷 **Um testador para cada controle**

O **Test controller** acende ao vivo cada botão, direção, analógico e gatilho, com um desenho para os joypads Xbox, PlayStation, GameCube, Super Nintendo, NES, Dreamcast, Saturn, Mega Drive, Neo Geo CD e Nintendo 64, e para os painéis de arcade. Segure Start para sair.

🇺🇸 **A tester for every controller**

**Test controller** lights up every button, direction, stick and trigger live, with a drawing for Xbox, PlayStation, GameCube, Super Nintendo, NES, Dreamcast, Saturn, Mega Drive, Neo Geo CD and Nintendo 64 pads, and for arcade panels. Hold Start to leave.

🇧🇷 **OpenBOR no RetroArch**

Os `.pak` do OpenBOR agora abrem no core **AnyBOR**, com 322 perfis de motor e a escolha automática do perfil de cada pak. O core é instalado na primeira vez que você abre um jogo de OpenBOR (uns 5 MB), e `~/roms/openbor` virou um atalho para a pasta dele, com os seus jogos movidos para lá sem sobrescrever nada. A imagem acima é o Moonblade, no gabinete.

🇺🇸 **OpenBOR in RetroArch**

OpenBOR `.pak` files now open in the **AnyBOR** core, with 322 engine profiles and an automatic choice of the profile for each pak. The core installs the first time you open an OpenBOR game (about 5 MB), and `~/roms/openbor` is now a shortcut to its folder, with your games moved there and nothing overwritten. The picture above is Moonblade, on the cabinet.
