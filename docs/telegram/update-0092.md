🇺🇸 **FliperOS update 92: the three songs now come with FliperOS** 🎶

The frontend's songs (BurtySounds - Retrowave, Musinova - Information Flow and kaazoom - Looking For A Way Back, from Pixabay) now come with FliperOS, distributed with the authors' authorization. The credits are in the README.

- Each one is copied to `~/media/fliperos-sounds/music` the first time the Setup or the frontend opens: a cabinet with no songs starts with music, and the first one to play is Retrowave.
- A song that is already there is left as it is, and one you delete does not come back.
- The update brings about 14 MB, and the 0.27 ISO has them too.

This corrects what the update 91 post said: the songs no longer have to be put there by hand.
